MoleculeAgent: From Biological Goals
to Evaluated Molecular Designs

Aditya Motwani  ·  Yeshwanth Govindarajan  ·  Haotong Xin  ·  Lei Li

Language Technologies Institute, Kenneth C. Griffin School of Computer Science, Carnegie Mellon University

Presented at the MLDD Symposium, Broad Institute.

Overview

Given a biological objective in natural language, MoleculeAgent plans a design campaign, runs the design and evaluation tools, simulates the candidates it keeps, and records the evidence behind every decision. A supervisor agent routes work to specialised planning, design, evaluation and simulation subagents, which reach their tools through MCP servers; a recovery controller catches failed steps and replans rather than restarting the campaign.
